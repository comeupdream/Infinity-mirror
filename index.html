SRC · ABOUT ・ 工房

Infinity Mirror Works

A one-bench lightworks. We split OEM housings, build the mirror stack by hand, and re-seam them tighter than the factory did — because a lamp you can fall into should never fog. This site runs the same programs our controllers ship with; what you're looking at is the product.

  • Blueprint-first
    Geometry drawn and animated before a single cut — approve the print, then we build it.
  • Chassis-honest
    Fitment badges need positive evidence. If your chassis isn't listed, it's a commission, not a maybe.
  • Street-aware
    Red / flashing show modes ship on a separate circuit — DOT lamps stay DOT.
